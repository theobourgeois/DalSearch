Systems Programming

Kalyaniwalla N.3 credit hours, 19 of 120 seats left this term
Lec 01 and Lab B03 selected

This course presents tools, techniques, and concepts for systems programming. Students will be exposed to assembly and will be introduced to the C programming language. The course also discusses memory hierarchies, performance measurement, exception control flow, and performance related issues. Students will be involved in a significant amount of low-level programming.
